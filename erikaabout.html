My name is Erika Novitsky.

I am a BFA student at the University of Southern California. I focus on illustration, comics, and narrative art.

I am affiliated with creative communities such as USC Roski and USC VNS, as well as Ryman Arts.
Due to my Japanese-American background, I take inspiriation from both Western and East Asian media.

I grew up reading manga and comics and I am currently en route to one day publishing my own stories.
Outside of art, I have other creative hobbies that gets not only my brain moving, but also my body.

I do my best to find art in everything I do.
